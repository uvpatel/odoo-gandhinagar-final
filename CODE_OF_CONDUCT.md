# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone contributing to PeoplePay360.

We value technical discussion, constructive disagreement, and different perspectives.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

* Be respectful and professional.
* Communicate constructively.
* Provide useful technical feedback.
* Accept different opinions and approaches.
* Focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than individuals.
* Help maintain a safe and inclusive project environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Intimidation or threats.
* Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
* Sharing private information without permission.
* Trolling or sustained bad-faith interaction.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project unsafe or hostile.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers.

For security-related concerns, follow the process described in `SECURITY.md`.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including warnings, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or removal from project participation.

The goal is to maintain a professional and productive engineering environment.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, discussions, pull requests, issues, and other project-related communication spaces.
